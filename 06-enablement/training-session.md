# Training session — 10 minutes, for the practice's office manager

<!-- TODO(evening 7): session plan, then record it (link the Loom here). -->

1. **What it does** (2 min) — watches your client list on Companies House, flags what's due,
   drafts the chasing emails, builds your Monday list.
2. **What it will never do** (1 min) — send anything. Every email is yours to approve first.
3. **How to review drafts** (4 min) — approve / edit / reject, why rejection reasons matter,
   reading the citations.
4. **When to distrust it** (2 min) — the deadline numbers come straight from Companies House and
   are safe; the *prose* is a draft, so read it like a junior's work.
5. **Where to get help** (1 min) — the runbook, and what "degraded" looks like.
