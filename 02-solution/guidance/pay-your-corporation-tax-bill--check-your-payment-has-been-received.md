title: Pay your Corporation Tax bill — Check your payment has been received
source_url: https://www.gov.uk/pay-corporation-tax/check-your-payment-has-been-received
fetched_on: 2026-11-03
---

Check your [HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC) online account](https://www.tax.service.gov.uk/gg/sign-in?continue=/corporation-tax/&origin=CORPTAX-frontend) to see if your payment has been received. It should be updated within a few days of HMRC receiving the payment.
  *[HMRC]: HM Revenue and Customs
