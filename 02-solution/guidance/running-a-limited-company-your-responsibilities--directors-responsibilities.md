As a director of a limited company, you must:

  * follow the company’s rules, shown in its [articles of association](/limited-company-formation/memorandum-and-articles-of-association)
  * [keep company records](/running-a-limited-company/company-and-accounting-records) and [report certain information](/running-a-limited-company/company-changes-you-must-report)
  * [prepare your annual accounts](/annual-accounts)
  * [complete your Company Tax Return](/guidance/the-company-tax-return-guide)
  * [file your accounts and your Company Tax Return](/prepare-file-annual-accounts-for-limited-company/file-your-accounts-and-company-tax-return)
  * tell other shareholders if you might personally benefit from a transaction the company makes
  * pay [Corporation Tax](/corporation-tax)



You can [hire other people](/tax-help) to manage some of these things day-to-day (for example, an accountant) but you’re still legally responsible for your company’s records, accounts and performance.

You may be fined, prosecuted or [disqualified from being a company director](/company-director-disqualification) if you do not meet your responsibilities.

Contact your professional adviser or [trade association](https://www.taforum.org/membership/member-directory/) to find out more.
