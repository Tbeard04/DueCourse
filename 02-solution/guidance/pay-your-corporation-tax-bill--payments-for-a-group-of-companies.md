If your company is in a group, you can pay Corporation Tax under a [Group Payment Arrangement](/corporation-tax-group-payment-arrangements).

HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC) will send you your Corporation Tax payment reference number.
  *[HMRC]: HM Revenue and Customs
