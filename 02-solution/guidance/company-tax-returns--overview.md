Your company or association must file a Company Tax Return if you get a ‘notice to deliver a Company Tax Return’ from HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC).

You must still send a return if you make a loss or have no Corporation Tax to pay.

You do not send a Company Tax Return if you’re [self-employed](/working-for-yourself) as a sole trader or in a partnership - but you must send a [Self Assessment return](/self-assessment-tax-returns).

## Filing your return

You should [file your return online](/government/publications/corporation-tax-commercial-software-suppliers).

You can only [file a return by post](/government/publications/corporation-tax-company-tax-return-ct600-2015-version-3) if you:

  * have a [reasonable excuse](/tax-appeals/reasonable-excuses)
  * are filing in Welsh



Find out about [filing company accounts and tax returns if you previously used the HMRC online service](/guidance/closure-of-the-service-to-file-your-company-accounts-and-tax-return).

## What it involves

When you file your tax return, you work out your:

  * profit or loss for Corporation Tax (this is different from the profit or loss shown in your annual accounts)
  * Corporation Tax bill



You can either [get an accountant](/appoint-tax-agent) to prepare and file your tax return or do it yourself.

## Deadlines

The deadline for your tax return is 12 months after the end of the [accounting period](/corporation-tax-accounting-period) it covers. You’ll have to pay a [penalty for late filing](/company-tax-returns/penalties-for-late-filing) if you miss the deadline.

There’s a separate deadline to [pay your Corporation Tax bill](/pay-corporation-tax). It’s usually 9 months and one day after the end of the accounting period.
