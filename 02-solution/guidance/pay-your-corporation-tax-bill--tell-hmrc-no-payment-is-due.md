---
title: Pay your Corporation Tax bill — Tell HMRC no payment is due
source_url: https://www.gov.uk/pay-corporation-tax/tell-hmrc-no-payment-due
fetched_on: 2026-11-03
---

Tell HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC) if you have nothing to pay. HMRC will send you payment reminders if you do not.

You must tell HMRC by filling in the [‘nil to pay’ form](http://www.hmrc.gov.uk/gds/payinghmrc/ct-nil.htm).

You must still [file your company tax return](https://www.tax.service.gov.uk/gg/sign-in?continue=/corporation-tax/&origin=CORPTAX-frontend).
  *[HMRC]: HM Revenue and Customs
